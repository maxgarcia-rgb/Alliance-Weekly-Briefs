📖 Metric Glossary
Integration GAS
Gross Ad Spend attributable to a specific integration (Shopify, HubSpot, etc.). Deduped at campaign level: if a campaign runs on 2 integrations simultaneously, each gets 50% of that campaign's GAS.
Overall Account GAS
Total Gross Ad Spend from an SFDC account across all campaigns, regardless of integration. Source: Snowflake account-level GAS file.
QTD Integration GAS
Deduped integration GAS accumulated from April 1 through the data pull date. This is the known actuals anchor for the model.
Model Call — Integration GAS
The model's full-quarter Q2 2026 projection for integration GAS. = QTD actual + projected remaining days (using daily pace × seasonality factor). Blended 60/40 with rep integration GAS estimate where available.
Rep Q2 Overall Acct FC
The rep's Q2 2026 forecast for total account GAS (not integration-specific). Sourced from the SFDC account-level forecast pull.
Integration GAS Est. from Rep FC
Estimated integration GAS derived from the rep's overall account forecast × the account's trailing 2Q integration ratio (deduped int GAS ÷ total account GAS).
Watch List
Accounts that had integration activity in Q1 2026 but have not yet appeared in Q2 2026. Model assigns probability weights based on consistency score, Q2 2025 history, and trajectory.
Not Yet Active Q2 — Integration GAS
Sum of Q1 2026 integration GAS from watch list accounts. Represents integration revenue at risk if these accounts don't reactivate in Q2.
New Business QTD — Integration GAS
Integration GAS in Q2 2026 from accounts that had $0 integration activity in Q1 2026. Quarterly cohort definition — accounts that reactivated after being dormant are counted here.
Seasonality Factor
Q2/Q1 integration GAS ratio from prior years. HubSpot and Snowflake use this; Shopify and Klaviyo use linear (2025 was ramp year, no reliable Q2/Q1 seasonal comp).
Trajectory
Direction of an account's integration spend over the last 3+ quarters. Growing = last quarter >10% above first; Declining = >10% below; Stable = within 10%.
Forecast Confidence Score
0–100 score reflecting model reliability. Inputs: % quarter elapsed (35%), % model from active accounts (30%), historical depth in quarters (20%), inverse watch/gap reliance (15%). Penalties applied for single-year seasonality (Shopify) and limited history (<3Q).
WoW Alliance Forecast Movement
Week-over-week change in the integration model call. Reflects updated QTD pacing as more days of data accumulate.
WoW Rep Forecast Flags
Rep overall account forecasts that moved ≥20% AND ≥$25K week over week on accounts with active integration history. Shown at integration account level — flags signal potential upside or risk to integration GAS.
